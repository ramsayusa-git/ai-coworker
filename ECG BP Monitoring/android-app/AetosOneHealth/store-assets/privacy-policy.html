Privacy Policy — Aetos One Health

Aetos Tech Labs · Last updated 13 September 2026

Aetos One Health does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything the app records stays on your phone.

The app is built without the Android INTERNET permission. It is not able to send data anywhere, to us or to anyone else, even by mistake. This is verifiable in the app's manifest.

What the app stores

Aetos One Health connects over Bluetooth to health devices you already own and records what they report:

  • ECG sessions: heart rate over time, beat counts, signal quality and session duration
  • Blood-pressure readings: systolic, diastolic and pulse
  • Profile names and any note you add, so several people can share one device
  • Calibration offsets you set

All of it is written to a private database inside the app's own storage area, which no other app on your phone can read.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDK
  • No advertising identifier, device identifier, or location data
  • No servers of ours are contacted, because there are none

Permissions, and why each one is needed

  • Bluetooth scan and connect — to find and talk to your ECG sensor and blood-pressure cuff. The scan permission is declared with neverForLocation, so the app cannot derive your location from it.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only) — older versions of Android require this permission before any app is allowed to scan for Bluetooth devices. The app never reads your location.
  • Notifications — to tell you when a reading has been recorded.
  • Foreground service — to keep the Bluetooth connection alive while the screen is off, so a measurement is not cut short.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose. Tapping Export creates a CSV or PDF and hands it to Android's share sheet, where you pick the destination — email, a messaging app, your own file storage. Until you do that, and to whatever you choose, nothing is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete any individual reading or session inside the app. To remove everything at once, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings; the database goes with it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

If you have Android backup enabled, your readings may be included in your own device backup. That backup is governed by Google's terms and your own Google account settings, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that would identify anyone.

Not a medical device

Aetos One Health displays and records readings from consumer Bluetooth devices. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it should not be used to make a medical decision on its own. Discuss any concern about a reading with a clinician.
