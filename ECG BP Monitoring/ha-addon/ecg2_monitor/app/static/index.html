Calibration

Align the heart rate with a reference. Compare the rate shown here against a reading you trust — a pulse oximeter, a manual count over 60 seconds — and nudge the offset until they agree. It is stored per profile and applied when a value is displayed or published; the database always keeps the rate the detector actually measured, so changing the offset later re-aligns your whole history rather than corrupting it. The RR interval is left uncalibrated, because it is a directly measured time between beats.

Clinical notice

Not a medical device. This dashboard is for monitoring and experimentation. The heart rate shown is computed by this add-on's own QRS detector; the device's own reported HR is displayed separately and is unreliable when the signal is noisy. Do not use any of it for diagnosis — check the signal quality tag before reading anything into a number.
