3️⃣ User Workflows

Three primary user journeys define the platform's core interactions: patient consultation booking and participation, doctor consultation management, and administrative oversight.

👤 Patient Workflow: Booking to Follow-up

1

Discovery & Registration

Patient signs up via email/phone, completes profile, and creates family linked accounts (optional). Keycloak handles identity with DPDPA consent collection.

2

Doctor Search & Booking

Patient browses doctors by specialty, checks availability, reads ratings, and books appointment. Automated reminder SMS/push sent 24h and 1h before slot.

3

Pre-Consultation Device Setup

Patient pairs mobile app with BLE devices (BP monitor, ECG, glucose). System tests connection. Android app captures baseline vitals.

4

Live Consultation

Patient joins Jitsi video room 5min before slot. Doctor sees live vital signs streamed over WebSocket (separate from Jitsi media). Patient can share readings in real-time.

5

Prescription & Follow-up

Doctor issues e-prescription in-app. Patient receives prescription via SMS/email. System triggers pharmacy delivery booking and refill reminders. Records auto-saved to FHIR + ABDM.

👨‍⚕️ Doctor Workflow: Schedule to Reporting

1

Credentialing & Onboarding

Doctor registers with NMC number. Admin verifies credentials via NMC database API. Doctor added to organization with role-based access control (RBAC).

2

Schedule Management

Doctor sets availability calendar (recurring/one-time slots). System syncs to patient-facing calendar. Integration with Google Calendar (optional).

3

Live Consultation Interface

Doctor joins Jitsi meeting. Dashboard shows patient vitals live in a panel (ECG waveform, BP trend, glucose, O2). AI Scribe listens and drafts notes in real-time.

4

E-Prescription & Decision Support

Doctor issues medications from drug database (with drug interactions checked by AI). Attaches imaging/reports. System suggests dosages based on patient history and comorbidities (AI-assisted, doctor approves).

5

Reporting & Analytics

Doctor dashboard shows daily consults, ratings, revenue. Reports exportable to PDF. Peer benchmarking anonymized. Compliance audit trail available.

⚙️ Admin Workflow: Oversight & Control

1

Organization Setup

Super-admin creates organization (clinic/hospital), sets billing model (per-consult / subscription), configures branding. Assigns org admins.

2

Doctor & Patient Management

Org admin adds doctors (bulk upload CSV), verifies NMC certs, assigns specialties/departments. Manages patient access rules (age restrictions, referral gates).

3

Compliance Monitoring

Admin dashboard shows DPDPA consent status per patient, audit logs of all record access, recording compliance (encryption verified). Generates compliance reports monthly.

4

Billing & Revenue

System tracks consults, applies pricing rules (org-specific), integrates with payment gateway. Admin sees revenue dashboards, invoices, and payout schedules.

5

Incident Response

Admin receives alerts on failed consultations, payment errors, security events. Can view detailed logs, contact support, and escalate issues.

5️⃣ Data Flow Architecture

Understanding how patient data, consultation records, and device vitals flow through the system with proper encryption, audit logging, and compliance boundaries.

Key Data Flow Principles

🔐 Separation of Concerns

Media Plane (Jitsi): Video/audio streams flow directly through Jitsi, encrypted end-to-end. Doctor app never receives raw video.

Data Plane (WebSocket): Vital signs, messages, and records flow through custom WebSocket gateway with database-first persistence model.

📊 Real-time Vitals

Device readings captured every 5 seconds on Android, buffered locally, streamed to WebSocket gateway, pushed to doctor dashboard with <2s latency. Database records every reading for 7-year compliance retention.

📋 Record Export

Every consultation auto-generates FHIR R4, HL7 v2.5, and ABDM bundles. Patients can export as PDF, CSV, JSON, or FHIR. All exports encrypted and audit-logged.

🔒 Encryption & Audit

All patient data encrypted AES-256-GCM at rest. TLS 1.3 in transit. Every access logged with user ID, timestamp, action, and reason. DPDPA breach notification automatic if >1000 records exposed.

🏥 EHR/HIS Integration

Patient Demographics: Pull from existing hospital HIS via HL7, sync bidirectional

Regulatory Milestones & Timelines

DPDPA 2023: Consent Manager Registration (14 Nov 2026) ▼

Deadline: 14 Nov 2026

Action: Register Aetos One Medical Hub as a Data Consent Manager with DPA (Data Protection Authority) once established.

Requirement: Privacy impact assessment (DPIA), consent ledger infrastructure, breach notification system operational.

Status: Consent ledger schema drafted, ready for registration application 60 days before deadline.

ABDM Functional Testing & Safe-to-Host (Phase 3 gate) ▼

Scope: ABDM sandbox exit requires three gates: functional testing by NHA-empanelled agency (FIME India, Suma Soft, Tata Communications), Safe-to-Host certificate from STQC/CERT-In-empanelled agency, and HFR registration of partner facilities.

Estimated Cost: ₹5-10 lakhs per certification

Timeline: 6-8 weeks (before Phase 3 launch)

Status: Quoted FIME India, integration tests passing 95%.

CDSCO Medical Device Software Classification (AI Agents) ▼

Scope: CDSCO 21 July 2026 guidance requires AI agents to be classified as medical device software (Class A-D). Rhythm Screener agent = Class C (moderate risk).

Requirements: SBOM (software bill of materials), cybersecurity plan, Algorithm Change Protocol, post-market surveillance.

Timeline: Registration before clinical deployment of agents (Phase 4)

Status: SBOM prepared, cybersecurity plan drafted, awaiting regulatory pathway clarification.

ISO 27001:2022 Certification (Planned Phase 3) ▼

Scope: Information Security Management System (ISMS) certification to demonstrate security maturity.

Assessment: Third-party audit covering control implementation, effectiveness, incident response, continuous improvement.

Timeline: Audit in Q2 2027, certificate valid 3 years

Status: ISMS documented, internal audit scheduled Q4 2026.

NHCX Claims Exchange Integration (Phase 4+) ▼

Scope: NHCX (National Health Claims Exchange) went live June 2026 for insurance claims. Future integration to enable automated claims submission.

Scope: Telconsultation coverage under PMJAY, health insurance, and corporate plans.

Timeline: Phase 4 (post-MVP)

Status: NHCX API documentation reviewed, integration design pending.

🎯 The Unoccupied Position

Live device readings streamed into a live consult is not a product anyone in this survey ships. The device companies (Dozee, Tricog) have no ABDM integration and no public APIs. The clinic-SaaS companies (Eka Care, HealthPlix, MocDoc) have no device layer. The teleconsult platforms (Practo, Apollo, 1mg) have neither. That gap is the entire wedge — and the Hub loses if it is positioned as "another AI clinic EMR", because that is a simultaneous fight with Eka Care (90,000 doctors, ₹2,999/mo) and Arogyam.ai (₹1,099/mo).

9.7 Threat Assessment

🔴 Eka Care — Highest Threat

90,000 doctors, NHA-approved, ambient scribe already at 1M sessions, and the only mature public API in the market (including an MCP server). Competing on "AI clinic EMR" is a direct fight we do not currently win.

Their gap: device integration — only Pillo Health is listed. That is where we attack.

🟠 Arogyam.ai — Price Pressure

₹1,099/mo entry with voice-to-SOAP and nine named agents resets buyer price expectations for 1–6 doctor clinics. Early-stage and unproven, but it anchors the low end.

Their gap: "ABDM-ready" is a readiness claim, not a certified milestone; no device layer.

🟡 eSanjeevani — Free Substitute

Gives away the nurse-assisted consult flow at 43 crore consults. Any state or public-health deal competes with it directly.

Do not compete on that flow alone — compete on live device data, private multi-branch org management, and a record spanning home and clinic.

🟡 KareXpert — Compliance Benchmark

The only vendor publicly claiming ABDM M1+M2+M3 certification, with Jio backing and Medical IoT already in the stack. Sets the enterprise procurement bar.

Implication: our ABDM milestone path needs budget and lead time for functional testing and the Safe-to-Host certificate.

🟢 Bahmni — Build-vs-Buy

Zero licence cost, offline-capable, on-premise. Any customer with an engineering team can deploy it.

Our answer must be better than "we wrote our own OpenMRS" — it has to be the device loop and the managed compliance posture.

🟢 Device Cohort — Silos

Dozee and Tricog hold the regulatory clearances we lack, but ship no ABDM integration and no open API. They are data islands.

Opportunity: integrate rather than out-build — be the platform their readings land in.
