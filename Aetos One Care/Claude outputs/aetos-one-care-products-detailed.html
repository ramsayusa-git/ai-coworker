Frequently Asked Questions

Are consultations confidential? ▼

Yes, all consultations are completely confidential. Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We comply with DPDPA 2023 and HIPAA-equivalent standards.

Can I cancel an appointment? ▼

Yes, you can cancel up to 2 hours before the consultation. Cancellations are refunded to your wallet instantly. Late cancellations (within 2 hours) are not refunded.

What if I can't attend the consultation? ▼

You can reschedule up to 2 hours before the consultation without any charges. If you miss the appointment, you forfeit the consultation fee.
