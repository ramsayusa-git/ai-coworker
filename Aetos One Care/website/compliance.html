Trust & compliance

The 2026 rulebook, read properly.

CDSCO’s final Medical Device Software guidance landed in July 2026 and classifies by function, not by disclaimer. DPDPA obligations are on a firm calendar. ABDM certification is a paid, gated process. Here is exactly where we stand.

🧱

CDSCO — software as a device

The July 2026 guidance classifies by what the software does. Our screening-scope labelling is enforced in product, a predetermined change control plan sits with each model, and rhythm classification stays behind a feature flag until cleared.

📜

DPDPA 2023

Consent is an object with a purpose, an expiry and a revocation path. Data-principal rights — access, correction, erasure — are product features, not support tickets.

🌐

ABDM

ABHA creation and linking, HIP/HIU consent flows and HFR-registered facilities. Milestones M1 to M3 are on the roadmap with sandbox exit, functional testing and the Safe-to-Host certificate budgeted rather than assumed.

🔑

Security

AES-256-GCM at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, RBAC enforced in the query layer by join, and Indian data residency by default.

📊

Audit & retention

Every record access carries a user, a record, an action, a timestamp and a reason code. Monthly partitions, 12-month regulatory retention, compression at seven days.

⚖️

Practitioner-anchored

There is no standalone licensing regime for telemedicine platforms in India — regulation attaches to the practitioner. Every clinical output is attributed to a registered doctor who signs it.
