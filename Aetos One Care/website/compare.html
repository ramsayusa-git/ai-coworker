What the table says

Four findings we did not want to be true.

🤹

Eka Care is ahead on AI

Ninety thousand doctors, an ambient scribe at a million sessions and the only mature public API in the market. If the scribe is all you need, they are the safer buy today.

💰

Arogyam.ai resets the price floor

₹1,099 a month for voice-to-SOAP and nine agents changes what a small clinic expects to pay. Any pricing above that needs a reason attached.

🏛️

eSanjeevani gives away the assisted consult

Forty-three crore consultations, free. Competing on the nurse-assisted flow alone is competing with the Government of India.

🔧

Bahmni is the build-vs-buy floor

Zero licence cost, offline, on-premise. Any customer with engineers can deploy it — so “we wrote our own OpenMRS” is not an answer.

Only four vendors in the entire Indian survey publish a price.

Practo Ray, Eka Care, Arogyam.ai and Clinicea. Everyone else quotes. We publish, because an information vacuum is a trust problem, not a negotiating advantage.
