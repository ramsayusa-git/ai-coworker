Why this matters

Portability is the feature, not the footnote.

Most Indian health apps will happily store your history and quietly make it hard to leave. We publish export in six formats because a record you cannot take with you is not your record.

How your data is handled

What leaves with you

  • ●Every consultation note, signed and timestamped
  • ●Raw and interpreted vitals, including ECG segments
  • ●Prescriptions with dosage, duration and issuing doctor
  • ●Lab results as structured FHIR observations
  • ●Your ABHA care-context links, intact
