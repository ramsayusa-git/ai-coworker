One loop: capture, stream, decide, persist.

The architecture exists to keep a physiological reading trustworthy from the sensor on a patient’s chest to the signed record a specialist reads six months later.

Four lanes: edge capture, the realtime signal bus, stateless services, and the record. Nothing crosses a lane without an authenticated room and an active consent artefact.

01 · Capture

Signal quality decided at the edge

A bad trace is worse than no trace. Every reading carries a quality score, the device’s calibration state and a device-signed timestamp before it is allowed on the bus.

  • ●BLE pairing with a device registry per organisation
  • ●On-device signing; clock skew corrected against server time
  • ●Signal-quality scoring per frame, surfaced to the doctor
  • ●Offline buffering with replay when the line returns
02 · Stream

Sub-second, in the same room as the video

Waveforms travel on a WebRTC data channel beside the media stream, so there is no second system to reconcile and no polling lag between what is said and what is seen.

  • ●SFU-based rooms with per-room authorisation
  • ●Vitals events and waveform frames on one ordered channel
  • ●Graceful degradation: vitals survive when video drops
  • ●Replay buffer for the last 10 minutes of any consult
03 · Decide

Assistive AI, scoped and attributable

The scribe drafts; the clinician signs. Decision support flags interactions and abnormal ranges. Nothing is presented as a diagnosis, and every suggestion is logged against the model version.

  • ●Ambient scribe drafting SOAP notes during the consult
  • ●Interaction and allergy checks at the point of prescribing
  • ●Queue triage by clinical urgency, not arrival order
  • ●Model version and prompt recorded on every output
04 · Persist

One record, and the patient can take it

Consult, waveform, note and prescription land as a single FHIR R4 encounter, linked to ABHA under a consent artefact the patient can revoke without calling anyone.

  • ●FHIR R4 observations for every vital and waveform segment
  • ●Six export formats including HL7 v2.5 and a QR share link
  • ●Care-context linking to ABHA via the ABDM bridge
  • ●Per-access audit rows with a reason code
