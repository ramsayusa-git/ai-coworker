ECG — ikinloop ECG2

Single-lead chest sensor over BLE. Continuous Lead II into the consult window, with per-frame quality scoring. Rhythm classification stays behind a feature flag pending CDSCO clearance.

🩸

Blood pressure

Standard BLE oscillometric cuffs. Systolic, diastolic and MAP written as FHIR observations with cuff size and posture captured alongside.

🦠

SpO₂ & pulse

Fingertip oximeters streaming saturation and perfusion index, with motion-artefact rejection before the reading reaches the doctor.

🍬

Glucose

BLE glucometers and CGM imports. Readings land on the same timeline as medication changes, so the trend and the prescription sit side by side.

📡

Device registry

Every device belongs to an organisation, carries a calibration date and a firmware version, and can be retired without orphaning its historical readings.

♻️

Replay & provenance

Ten minutes of rolling buffer per consult, plus a permanent provenance chain: which device, which firmware, which signal quality, which clinician saw it.

Integration posture

We would rather integrate your device than out-build it.

The RPM cohort has spent years on signal processing and regulatory files. Reproducing that is a bad use of everyone’s capital. What is missing is the interoperability layer — so that is what we build.
