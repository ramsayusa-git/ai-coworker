Why are you more expensive than Arogyam.ai?

Because the device layer is not free to run — registry, streaming, replay storage and provenance. If you do not need live vitals, their price is honestly the better deal.

Do patients pay?

No. Patient accounts, record access and export are free. Charging someone to read their own medical history is not a business model we want.

What happens to our data if we leave?

You get a full FHIR R4 export plus HL7 v2.5 interfaces and the raw waveform archive, within 30 days, at no charge. The exit plan is in the contract, not a goodwill gesture.
