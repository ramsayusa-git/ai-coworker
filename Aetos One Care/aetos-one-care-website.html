Frequently Asked Questions

Are all doctors on Aetos One Care verified and licensed? ▼
Yes, absolutely. Every doctor on our platform is NMC-registered (National Medical Commission) and verified for their credentials. We conduct rigorous background checks and maintain strict quality standards to ensure you receive consultations from qualified healthcare professionals only.
How is my personal health data protected? ▼
Your data is our priority. We use 256-bit military-grade encryption, comply with DPDPA 2023, and store all data on Indian servers. We never share your information with third parties without explicit consent. You have complete control over your health records.
How quickly can I get a consultation? ▼
Most consultations are available within 30 minutes. You can book with your preferred doctor or choose the next available specialist. During off-hours, emergency consultations are available 24/7 for urgent health concerns.
Are digital prescriptions valid for purchasing medicine? ▼
Yes, digital prescriptions issued by our doctors are legally valid across India. They're recognized by all pharmacies and have the same validity as paper prescriptions. You can either use our medicine delivery service or visit any pharmacy with the digital prescription.
Can I access my health records from the Ayushman Bharat system? ▼
Yes, Aetos One Care is integrated with ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission). You can link your Ayushman Bharat Health Account with us to access and share health records across India's healthcare ecosystem seamlessly.
