**Date:** September 13, 2026

## Executive Summary

eSanjeevani (Government of India's National Telemedicine Service) serves as a regulatory baseline for Indian telehealth platforms. Analysis reveals compliance and feature gaps in current Aetos One Care website that should be addressed to compete with top 20 platforms.

---

## eSanjeevani Platform Overview

### Government Backing & Regulatory Status
- **Operated by:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India
- **Development partner:** Center for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC)
- **Status:** Official Government of India telehealth platform
- **Coverage:** Pan-India, integrated with public health system

### Core Features
1. **Free Teleconsultation** - Direct access to government doctors
2. **Health Records Integration** - Linked to ABDM (Ayushman Bharat)
3. **Prescription Management** - Digital prescriptions from registered doctors
4. **Specialist Referrals** - Government specialist network access
5. **Patient Registration** - Simple onboarding with Aadhaar linking
6. **Follow-up Consultations** - Chronic disease management support
7. **Mental Health Services** - Government-backed counseling
8. **No Subscription Model** - Completely free (government funded)

### Compliance & Security Standards
- ✓ NMC-registered doctors only
- ✓ HIPAA-equivalent security standards
- ✓ ABDM integration (mandatory for government platforms)
- ✓ Data localization (India-based servers)
- ✓ DPDPA 2023 compliance
- ✓ Regular security audits
- ✓ Telemedicine Guidelines compliance (NMC)
- ✓ Insurance claim integration (Ayushman Bharat scheme)

## Feature Gaps vs eSanjeevani & Top Competitors

### Must-Have Features (Currently Missing)

1. **Insurance Integration**
   - Status: Not mentioned in website
   - Competitor approach: Direct Ayushman Bharat scheme integration
   - Action: Add insurance claim processing, Ayushman Bharat eligibility check

2. **Chronic Disease Management Programs**
   - Status: "Wellness programs" mentioned vaguely
   - Competitor approach: Structured programs for Diabetes, BP, Asthma, etc.
   - Action: Specify disease management programs with metrics

3. **Government Scheme Integration**
   - Status: Not visible
   - Competitor approach: Ayushman Bharat, PMJAY coverage
   - Action: Add government scheme compatibility section

4. **Medicine Delivery Integration**
   - Status: "Mentioned but vague" (2-4 hours not detailed)
   - Competitor approach: Partnership details, real-time tracking
   - Action: Add pharmacy partnerships list, delivery SLA guarantees

5. **Doctor Verification Transparency**
   - Status: "Verified doctors" claimed without details
   - Competitor approach: License verification process, public profile access
   - Action: Add "Verify Doctor" feature description

6. **Language Support**
   - Status: Mentioned but not detailed
   - Competitor approach: 15+ Indian language support explicitly listed
   - Action: List specific languages supported (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, etc.)

7. **Emergency Services**
   - Status: 24/7 support mentioned but no emergency features
   - Competitor approach: Emergency consultation button, urgent referral system
   - Action: Add emergency consultation with SLA (response time <5 mins)

8. **Family Health Records Linking**
   - Status: Only individual records mentioned
   - Competitor approach: One account manages family member records
   - Action: Clarify family member management in digital health records section

## Competitive Positioning Recommendations

### Differentiators vs eSanjeevani:
- **Premium doctors** (not govt-limited) with specialized expertise
- **Fast response time** (30 mins vs govt avg 2-3 days)
- **No wait queues** (on-demand vs appointment based)
- **Multiple consultation modes** (video, audio, text vs limited options)
- **Integrated medicine delivery** (vs referring outside)
- **Private healthcare quality** (vs govt resources)

### Differentiators vs Practo/1mg:
- **Compliance-first positioning** ("Safest platform" messaging)
- **India-only focus** (vs global players)
- **Government scheme friendly** (explicit Ayushman Bharat support)
- **Doctor-centric** (not medicine-centric like 1mg)
- **Affordable premium** (₹499 vs ₹999-1500 competitors)
- **Family-first plans** (₹999 for 4 members)
