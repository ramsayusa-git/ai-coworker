Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify if a doctor is NMC-registered? ▼
Every doctor's NMC license is automatically verified when they join Aetos One Care. You can click on any doctor's profile to see their registration details, license number, and specialization. We cross-check with the National Medical Commission database monthly to ensure validity.
Is my data really encrypted and DPDPA compliant? ▼
Yes, absolutely. We use 256-bit end-to-end encryption (military-grade security). All data is stored on Indian servers only (data localization). We're ISO 27001 certified and have completed a DPIA (Data Protection Impact Assessment) audit. You can access our compliance certifications and audit reports from the Trust & Security page.
How quickly can I get a consultation? ▼
Most consultations are available within 15-30 minutes. You can choose your preferred doctor or select "Next Available" for instant booking. During off-hours, our emergency specialists are available 24/7. For non-urgent concerns, you can also schedule consultations up to 30 days in advance.
Are digital prescriptions valid everywhere? ▼
Yes, digital prescriptions from our NMC-registered doctors are legally valid across India. All pharmacies accept them. You can either use our medicine delivery service (2-4 hour delivery in major cities) or visit any pharmacy with your digital prescription. The prescription is valid for 30 days from issuance.
How do I access my health records from Ayushman Bharat? ▼
Aetos One Care is fully integrated with ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission). You can link your Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) with Aetos One Care in settings. Once linked, your health records automatically sync across state hospitals and authorized healthcare providers using ABDM.
Which insurance schemes are supported? ▼
We support Ayushman Bharat PMJAY (₹5 lakh coverage) and most major health insurance plans. During consultation booking, you can provide your insurance details, and we'll verify eligibility. If eligible, you won't pay any consultation charges. For non-eligible treatments, you can pay out-of-pocket and claim reimbursement from your insurance.
Can my family members use the same account? ▼
Yes, with our Family Care plan (₹999/month), you can add up to 4 family members. Each person gets their own secure profile, health records, and doctor access. Parents can monitor children's consultations, and the primary account holder can manage permissions. Individual privacy is always maintained.
What makes Aetos One Care different from other platforms? ▼
We're built specifically for India with compliance at our core. Every platform claims security, but we're the only one with ISO 27001 certification and DPIA audit from day one. Our doctor verification is transparent (you can see their NMC license). We prioritize your rights under DPDPA—you can delete your data, access it anytime, and export it. No hidden terms. No data selling.
