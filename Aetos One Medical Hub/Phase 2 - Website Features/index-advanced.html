Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is my health data secure and private?

⌄

Yes. We're fully DPDPA 2023 compliant, use AES-256 encryption, and integrate with ABDM for secure data handling. Your data is yours.

How do I export my medical records?

⌄

Export in 6 formats: PDF (printable), CSV (spreadsheets), JSON (developers), FHIR R4 (healthcare systems), HL7 v2.5 (legacy EHR), or summary (email share).

Can I link family members?

⌄

Yes. Add up to 5 family members (based on plan) with role-based access controls and parental supervision for minors.

How fast is delivery for medicines?

⌄

Our pharmacy partners deliver within 2-4 hours in metro areas and 24 hours in regional areas. Track in real-time.

What payment methods do you accept?

⌄

We accept all major cards, UPI, digital wallets, and net banking. EMI options available for annual plans.
