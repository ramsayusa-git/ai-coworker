Improvement Lab

Find out WHY calls fail and stop the same problem coming back. Replay recorded calls through different speech engines, score real calls against an AI-drafted success checklist, and turn confirmed failures into permanent regression tests.

STT Lab Tab

Replay a recorded call through different speech-recognition engines and noise-cancellation settings, side-by-side with a 'best possible transcription' reference, to SEE what the agent misheard and which setup hears your callers best. Results compare setups against each other. Adding a misheard word to the agent's vocabulary is one click, and the change goes through the normal versioned agent save.

The Two Modes

'What the agent actually heard' streams the audio in real time through the same live pipeline your agent uses (streaming model, noise cancellation, time pressure to finalise words) — it shows what the agent WOULD have heard with that setup. 'Best possible transcription' sends the whole recording to the provider's offline service with no time pressure — that provider's accuracy CEILING on your audio. The gap between them is the diagnosis: great ceiling but poor live result means your live settings are costing you (fixable); a poor ceiling means the audio itself is the problem. Streaming-only models (e.g. Deepgram Flux) can only run as 'What the agent actually heard'. 'Caller words missed' is the fair comparison column (lower is better); 'Whole call' mixes both speakers — only compare rows with it; a '—' means the Lab wasn't confident which speaker was which and withholds the number.

Scheduled Runs

In the estimate popup, 'Schedule for later…' runs the replay after hours instead of now: once (pick a date), daily, or weekly, at a time in the dashboard's configured timezone, with an optional 'stop after N runs' spending cap. If the server is busy at the scheduled time (live call, another run, low memory) it retries for up to 4 hours, then reports why it couldn't run. Each run charges the estimated cost whether or not it succeeds; repeating schedules auto-pause after 3 failed runs in a row, and stop for re-confirmation if the run's cost changes. The Scheduled Runs card shows next fire time, cost per run, run count, status, and how the last run ended, with a Cancel button.

Noise Cancellation Tab

Find the noise-cancellation setting that hears your callers best. Pick an agent, tick several of its recorded calls (the newest 5 are pre-ticked, up to 10) and the settings to try — No noise cancellation, WebRTC APM, DTLN, ai.coustics (with its model and a strength each). The Lab replays every call through every setting with the agent's current speech engine held fixed, one call at a time in the background, and fills a grid: one row per call, one column per setting, each box showing how many of the caller's words came out wrong. Above the grid it names the winner on the calls EVERY setting completed (a paired comparison — the same audio under each treatment), needs at least 3 such calls and a 1-point lead, otherwise says 'too close to call'. 'Apply to agent' writes the winning setting to the agent in one click. Every box has a play button so you can hear exactly what the speech engine heard under that setting, next to the untreated audio ('Raw'). 'Voice kept', 'Noise kept', 'Dropouts' and 'ms/frame' are diagnostics only — they never decide the ranking. If the server is busy the sweep pauses with a Resume button; re-running a failed call charges it again. ai.coustics minutes are billed by ai.coustics and are not in the estimate.

Scoring Tab

The AI drafts a success checklist for each agent from its own prompt and tools — you review and approve it, then score batches of real calls to get a success rate. Failures land in a triage queue with the likely cause and the evidence shown inline; confirming a cause records it on the call and feeds the Improve tab. Verdicts are honest three-state (pass / fail / unknown) — ambiguous calls abstain instead of poisoning the rate.

Improve Tab

Turn confirmed failures into permanent regression tests: conversation failures become text simulation tests (you review the AI's draft first), and mishearing failures become audio regression tests (the call's audio is pinned and re-checked against a fixed reference after any speech-config change). 'Expand with AI' writes extra variations of a test for you to review and keep. 'Run all ready' runs every audio test one after another with a single upfront cost confirmation.

Costs

Nothing runs or spends money unless you click it, and every run shows an itemized estimate first. What the Lab spends is added to the original call's cost breakdown as an 'Improvement Lab' line — the same way AI Summary costs appear — so it shows up in your normal cost reports. Replay charges are recorded per attempt from the estimate (a run that fails still spent money); scoring, diagnosis, and AI-generation charges are actuals.

Good to Know

Audio replays run one at a time and pause while a real call is active. Backups keep your tests, checklists, and results — but not the pinned audio copies: a restored audio test on a new server will ask you to re-create it from a call, which is only possible if the original recording still exists there. Sub-user access is controlled by the Improvement Lab permission in User Management.
